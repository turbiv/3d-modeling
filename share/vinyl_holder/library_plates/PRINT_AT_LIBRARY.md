# Vinyl holder — print card (Ultimaker S3, Oodi / Pasila)

Copy this folder's `plate_2.stl` and `plate_6.stl` (and `plate_4.stl` for Pasila) to a USB stick.

## Plan for 20 holders
| Visit | File | Holders | Time (est.) | Book |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (test) | plate_2.stl | 2 | ~2.3 h | 3 h |
| 2 | plate_6.stl | 6 | ~7.0 h | 8 h, Oodi |
| 3 | plate_6.stl | 6 | ~7.0 h | 8 h, Oodi |
| 4 | plate_6.stl | 6 | ~7.0 h | 8 h, Oodi |

Pasila (6 h max): use `plate_4.stl` (~4.6 h) instead of `plate_6`.
Book **Oodi 3D-tulostin 1 (Musta PLA)** for black. One booking at a time.

## Cura settings
1. Printer: **Ultimaker S3**, nozzle **AA 0.4**, material **Ultimaker PLA (black)**.
2. Open the plate STL. **Don't rotate or rearrange it** — it is already floor-down with the flat backs facing left.
3. Profile: **Fast – 0.2 mm**.
4. Custom settings:
   - Walls → Wall Line Count: **3**
   - Walls → Z Seam Alignment: **User Specified**, Z Seam Position: **Left** (puts the seam on the hidden wall side)
   - Infill: **15 %**
   - Support: **off**
   - Build Plate Adhesion Type: **Skirt** (no brim)
5. Slice. Check the time is at least 45 min shorter than your booking; if not, use the next smaller plate.

## After printing
- Let the glass bed cool before taking the holders off — they release by themselves.
- Mounting: clean the wall with rubbing alcohol, stick one Command **medium** strip vertically on the back
  of the tall plate with the tab pointing down, press 30 s, wait 1 h before putting a record on.
- Take records out by lifting them up, not pulling toward you.
